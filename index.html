Rule 4 & Rule 7 Verification

This prototype contains zero static mock datasets. All data is either dynamically user-generated or fetched live from active open APIs:

  • Open-Meteo API: Live weather & precipitation metrics (`https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast`).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: Live NGO geolocation lookup (`https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search`).
  • Gemini 3 Flash AI API: Live food demand forecasting & redistribution strategy synthesis (`https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com`).
